Nabil Bank Limited — 71 / 100

Nepal’s oldest continuously operating private commercial bank. Perfect marks on liquidity, strong sector durability, and a governance record that is good without being spotless. The binding constraint is valuation: it trades at roughly 1.3–1.5 times the sector on both earnings and book value.

Chilime Hydropower — 62 / 100

A 22.1 MW run-of-river plant commissioned in 2003. Flawless dividend discipline, but a single completed plant has a hard growth ceiling, and the royalty step-up at year 16 reshapes the cost base on a fixed calendar in a way most retail models never account for. The plant was damaged in the 26 August 2026 Bhotekoshi flood and generation halted; the analysis predates that event and has not yet been rescored.
