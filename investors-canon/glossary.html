Circuit trap

Market

A share that keeps hitting its circuit limit day after day while very few shares change hands. When it turns, it can hit the lower limit day after day and you cannot sell at all. A fast rise on real news is not a trap by itself; the warning is limit hits plus tiny turnover plus a rumour.

In everyday terms A one-way bridge: easy to cross in, impossible to cross back when traffic turns.
