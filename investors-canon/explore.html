The Investor's Canon

The heart of the site: a complete book on investing in Nepal, written in the open and kept up to date as Nepal's rules and data change.

In plain words: most investing books are written for Wall Street. This one starts from what a Nepali reader already knows — Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) policy, NEPSE, remittances from Doha and Kuala Lumpur, the price of tomatoes at the local sabzi mandi — and builds up from there. No term is used before it is explained.

Reading tools on every page

Look at the top bar of any page in the book. These buttons work everywhere, on a phone or a computer.

SearchPress the Search button, or the / key, and type part of a chapter title, such as hydropower, tax or IPO. Enter opens the top result; Esc closes.
ListenThe headphones button reads the page aloud with your browser's voice — handy on a bus ride. Choose 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× or 2× speed and a voice.
नेपाली — read in NepaliOne tap translates the page into Nepali with Google Translate. It is machine translation, so check numbers against the English. Tap again for English.
Print or save as PDFThe printer button gives a clean copy without the buttons — like a photocopy for your study file. Choose “Save as PDF” to keep it.
Filter the contentsOn the contents page, type in the filter box to show only the chapters whose titles match.
Reading progressA thin rainbow bar at the very top fills up as you scroll, so you can see how much of a chapter is left.
“In this chapter” listEvery chapter opens with a list of its lessons. Tap one to jump straight to it.
Dates you can trustEach chapter shows when it was first published and when its facts were last verified. Recently revised chapters give the Bikram Sambat (BS) date too.
Previous and nextAt the end of each chapter, two buttons take you to the chapter before and after — like turning a page.
Day and nightThe book follows your phone's dark setting automatically. The home page has its own sun/moon button.
Volume coloursA coloured chip above each chapter title shows which volume you are in. Tap it to go back to that volume in the contents.

The Canon Score toolkit

The Canon Score is not just described in words. Its rules are published as files that anyone can download and run, so nobody has to take the numbers on trust.

Why publish the files? At a vegetable market, the Nepal Bureau of Standards and Metrology checks the shopkeeper's scale so that a kilo really is a kilo. These files are the book's scale, left out in the open to be checked. They run with Python 3 and nothing else to install.

Questions

Short answers to what new visitors usually ask.

Is the book free?

Yes. Every chapter is free to read online, with no account and no sign-up.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is teaching material. It explains how to think about Nepali shares; it does not tell anyone to buy or sell a particular share. Always do your own research and, if you need advice, speak to a licensed professional.

I am a complete beginner. Where do I start?

Open the Framework for the big picture, then How to Read This Book to pick a route, then begin with Chapter 0.1. Keep the Glossary open in another tab.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every page fits a phone screen, and the Listen button is useful when you cannot look at the screen.

Can I read it without internet?

Open the Whole Book page once on Wi-Fi and save it as a PDF with the print button. The PDF then works offline.

Does this site track me?

It counts visits, not people. Each page view and each tap on a main button (Listen, Search, नेपाली, Print, a data download) adds one to a counter at GoatCounter, so the author can see which chapters and tools are used. It uses no cookies, shows no ads and sells nothing. Like a gatekeeper with a hand-counter, it knows how many came in, not who you are. Nothing you type in Search or the contact form is sent.

How do I report a mistake?

Use the contact form. Corrections are recorded openly; Chapter 64's evidence register shows how.

Every chapter is published, and the Canon keeps being revised as Nepal's rules and data change. Have a question, correction, or a topic you want covered? Reach out via santoshkumaradhikari.com.np.
