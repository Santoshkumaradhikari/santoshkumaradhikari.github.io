Canon Score Engine

Type the figures from a company's own reports, and this page scores all sixteen parts of the Canon Score exactly as Chapter 64 (rubric 1.6.0) sets them out, applies the caps and the evidence grade, and shows how every point was reached.

What it does. The arithmetic and the rules, with no judgement of its own. It gives the same answers as the book's reference calculation: all 106 published test cases pass, and 6,000 random cases agree.

What it does not do. It does not fetch data. NEPSE, NRB and company reports have no official feed it could read reliably, and a wrongly copied figure looks just as certain as a right one. You find the figures; the engine scores them.

Where to find the figures. Audited annual reports and quarterly reports (company websites and NEPSE), NRB's monthly report for system figures, NIA for insurers, CDSC records for promoter holdings. Mark any figure you could not check, so the evidence grade stays honest.

How much could I hold?

The most you may hold is the smallest of the band's ceiling, the liquidity limit and the sector limit (Chapters 56 and 66). You can always buy less.
