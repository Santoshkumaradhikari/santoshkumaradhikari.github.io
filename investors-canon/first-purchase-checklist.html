The Complete First Purchase Checklist

Every check in Your First Share Purchase, in order, with nothing left out: 106 checks across eight steps, plus a score-and-size calculator and a decision record. Tick as you go; your ticks are saved in this browser for each company you study.

Gate Failing a gate ends the study, however good the rest looks. Part 1 gates mean stop and fix it first; company gates mean Reject.

Evidence gate Failing it means Not enough evidence: do not buy until the figures can be checked.

Wait gate Failing it means Wait: the business may be fine, but the price or the season is not.

Four possible answers for every company: Buy, Wait, Reject, Not enough evidence. Deciding not to buy is a valid decision.
