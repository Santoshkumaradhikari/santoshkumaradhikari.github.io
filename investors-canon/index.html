The Investor's Canon

A plain-English guide to reading a company's financial statements, understanding the credit cycles that move whole economies, and learning how the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) actually works underneath the ticker.

by Santosh Adhikari

🖋️ In progress — 3 of many chapters drafted

This book is being written chapter by chapter, out in the open, rather than released all at once. Every idea is explained the way you'd explain it to a curious friend over tea: no term used before it's defined, and no concept introduced before the last one has settled. Examples are drawn from things a Nepali reader already knows — remittances, NRB policy, NEPSE, the local sabzi mandi — so the ideas have somewhere familiar to land.
